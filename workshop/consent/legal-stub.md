# Consent — legal register (STUB · requires counsel review before first cohort)

> **Status: DRAFT STUB.** This register must be reviewed by qualified counsel
> (and, for a university deployment, the institution's ethics board) before
> any participant signs. It exists so the three-register structure (C19) is
> complete from day one; it is not yet legal advice or a binding instrument.

1. **Parties.** This Agreement is bilateral between the individual
   participant ("First Person") and the workshop-holder ("Second Party").
   Software agents, devices, and trust communities are expressly not parties
   (IEEE Std 7012-2025 posture).
2. **Offer.** Terms are proposed by the First Person from the workshop's
   standard registers; the Second Party may accept, make one counter-offer,
   or decline.
3. **Licence, per expression.** For each expression listed against the First
   Person's name in the Consent Ledger, the First Person grants the Second
   Party a revocable, non-exclusive licence limited to the recorded scope:
   (a) *corpus* — local machine-reading/grounding on air-gapped hardware
   within the workshop; (b) *export* — inclusion in the workshop's bound
   artefacts and/or federated wiki publication. No other use is licensed.
4. **Revocation.** The First Person may revoke per expression at any time;
   the Second Party shall delete the corpus copy, strike the ledger entry,
   and rebuild the machine context without it within [N] days. Copies already
   in printed artefacts or federated forks are governed by [to be drafted].
5. **No automated assessment of the person.** Machine outputs are not used as
   the assessment of record of the First Person.
6. **Records.** The Consent Ledger and steward log constitute the record of
   licences and processing; both are inspectable by the First Person.
7. **Governing law / institution-specific terms.** [To be completed with
   counsel / the partner institution.]
8. **Controlled-language rendering.** The structural content of clauses 2–4
   is also rendered as a Lexon controlled-natural-language contract
   (`lexon/consent_agreement.lex`), mechanically verified for internal
   coherence (`node lexon/check.mjs`), including the structural absence of
   any transfer route to the Second Party. In case of conflict between
   registers, the parties' recorded intent governs and all registers are
   corrected together.
