# The Fold, Read Aloud

*Submitted triptych t1 · second tongue.*

---

What you say to enter is what it will eat:
one saying, twice served — the key and the meat.

The lock here is the lesson; say it true
and the door goes as deep as the depth in you.

We feed it on Fridays, out loud, by name,
and the mirror gives back the room that came —

not my machine, not yours: a bowl
too wide for one hand. We carry it whole.

Understand, then speak; then the spoken feeds
the glass that returns you your own deep needs —

and round: what it shows you, you understand
better, and say better. Bowl in hand.
