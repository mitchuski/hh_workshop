# The Steward Protocol: A Working Note

*Submitted triptych t1 · third tongue. Formal register of the same
understanding the story dramatizes and the poem compresses.*

## 1. The role

Each week one participant stewards the shared model's training. The steward
holds the *process*, not the machine: the mage is co-held by the whole room
— a witness the group bears together, no member's property (C11).

## 2. Admission

Expressions are offered, never taken: a participant submits their own
material to the roster and no device or person is probed (C16). Each
expression enters the corpus only against a consent line signed by its
author in the ledger — the steward may never sign on another's behalf, and
the packing tool refuses any file the ledger does not cover, so a violated
consent cannot produce a result at all (C20).

## 3. The fold

The steward runs the weekly fold: consented expressions are packed into the
model's grounding context, and the resulting pack hash is read aloud to the
room and signed into the steward log. The model is thereby made of the
room's own words — a mirror of the cohort, deepening weekly (C12). Training
depth is staged: the good first pass is retrieval/context-grounding, run
now on any hardware; true weight fine-tuning is the perfect, adopted later
when hardware permits (C13).

## 4. Access

There are no logins. Access to the mage deepens as a participant expresses
understanding (C5); the lock and the lesson are one mechanism (C6). Each
admitted expression functions twice — as the participant's key and as the
mage's food (C7).

## 5. The loop

The cycle closes on itself: understand → express → the expression is
admitted (key and food) → the mage reflects the room's understanding back →
understanding deepens → recurse (C15). The steward's week is one turn of
that wheel, and the handover — log entry read aloud, key passed — is the
turn made visible.
